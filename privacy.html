This notice explains what personal information the HR Intake Portal (the "Service") handles, why, who can see it, and your choices. The Service is operated by [Legal entity name] ("we", "us"). We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws.

1. Who is responsible for your information

  • Your production decides what crew information to collect through the Service, why, and who on the production can see it. For that information, the production is responsible, and we handle it on its behalf, following its instructions.
  • We are responsible for account and sign-in information, and for running and securing the Service.

If you're crew, questions about what your production collects or how it uses it are best sent to your production office. You can always contact us too (see the end of this notice).

3. Why we use it

  • To sign you in, keep your account secure, and prevent misuse.
  • To let your production onboard you: send invites, collect and countersign forms, request and review documents, approve timesheets and invoices, issue paystubs and export payroll data.
  • To fill in forms for you from details you've already given, so you don't have to retype them.
  • To send service emails, such as sign-in codes and invitations.
  • To keep an audit trail, investigate problems, and meet legal obligations.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising or to build marketing profiles. The Service has no advertising or analytics trackers.

4. Who can see it

  • Your production's administrators can see the information and documents you provide to that production. Department heads can see timesheets for the departments they review. People on your production you list as approvers see the documents they approve.
  • Other productions can't see your information. If you work on more than one production, each only sees what you provide to it.
  • Our platform administrators can see the list of productions and their administrators, account emails, and the audit log, to operate and support the Service.
  • Service providers that run parts of the Service for us (below), only as needed to provide it.
  • Authorities, if required by law, or to protect someone's safety or the Service's security.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The Service stores your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in, and a few preferences, such as which section you last opened and whether you hid the getting-started checklist. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. How long we keep it

  • Production information is kept while the production is active and its administrators keep it. Administrators can remove crew, documents and invites. When a production is deleted, its records and files are deleted.
  • If you're removed from your only production, your account may be deleted with it.
  • Backup codes are kept until you replace or use them. Passkey sign-in challenges expire after 5 minutes.
  • [Retention period for productions after wrap, and for backups, to be decided.]

8. Security

  • Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with our database provider, which encrypts it at rest.
  • Access rules in the database keep each production's information separate, and files are stored privately, not at public addresses.
  • Sign-in uses one-time email codes or passkeys instead of passwords. Production administrators must also use two-step sign-in.
  • Changes are recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll notify affected people and the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or ask questions about how it's handled. For information a production collected, we'll work with that production on your request.
  • You can withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual limits. This may mean you can't keep using the Service for that production.
  • You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or your provincial privacy commissioner.

10. Children

The Service is for people old enough to work legally on a production. Productions engaging minors are responsible for getting a parent's or guardian's consent where required.

11. Changes

When we update this notice, we'll change the version date above and ask you to review it the next time you sign in.
